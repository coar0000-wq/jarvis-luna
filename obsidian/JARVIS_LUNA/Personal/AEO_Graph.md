## What is AEO?

[[AI Engine Optimization (AEO)]] is the practice of optimizing content and websites to be recommended by AI-powered search engines, chatbots, and recommendation systems instead of appearing in traditional link-based search results.

### Why AEO Matters
[[Shift in Search]]:
- [[ChatGPT Integration]]: AI in search
- [[Claude Recommendations]]: AI suggestions
- [[Google AI Overviews]]: AI-generated summaries
- [[Traditional SEO Declining]]: Links matter less
- [[New Ranking Factors]]: AI-specific signals
- [[Brand Vulnerability]]: Competition increases
- [[First-Mover Advantage]]: Get ahead now
- [[Future Proofing]]: Stay relevant

### AEO vs. SEO
[[Key Differences]]:
- **SEO**: Optimized for algorithms
- **AEO**: Optimized for AI recommendations
- **SEO**: Technical factors matter
- **AEO**: Content quality paramount
- **SEO**: Keywords important
- **AEO**: Intent and meaning matter
- **SEO**: Backlinks critical
- **AEO**: Authority through content
