## What is Digital Marketing?

[[Digital Marketing]] is the use of digital channels, platforms, and technologies to promote products, services, and brands to target audiences.

### Key Definition
- [[Reach]] global audience online
- [[Cost Effective]] compared to traditional marketing
- [[Measurable]] results and ROI tracking
- [[Data Driven]] decision making

## Digital Marketing Pillars

### 1. Search Engine Optimization
[[SEO]] (Search Engine Optimization):
- [[Keyword Research]]: Find profitable keywords
- [[On-Page SEO]]: Optimize website content
- [[Off-Page SEO]]: Build backlinks and authority
- [[Technical SEO]]: Improve site structure
- [[Local SEO]]: Target geographic locations

### 2. Search Engine Marketing
[[SEM]] (Paid Search):
- [[Google Ads]]: Display and search campaigns
- [[Bid Management]]: Maximize ROI on clicks
- [[Ad Copy]]: Compelling headlines and descriptions
- [[Landing Pages]]: Convert traffic to customers
- [[PPC]] (Pay-Per-Click): Cost-per-click model

### 3. Social Media Marketing
[[Social Media Marketing]]:
- [[Facebook Marketing]]: Largest audience platform
- [[Instagram Marketing]]: Visual content platform
- [[LinkedIn Marketing]]: B2B professional network
- [[Twitter Marketing]]: Real-time engagement
- [[TikTok Marketing]]: Short-form video content
- [[YouTube Marketing]]: Video marketing platform
- [[Social Engagement]]: Comments, likes, shares
- [[Influencer Marketing]]: Partner with creators

### 4. Content Marketing
[[Content Marketing]]:

- [[Podcasts]]: Audio content
- [[Email Newsletters]]: Subscriber engagement
- [[Content Strategy]]: Planning content calendar
- [[Content Distribution]]: Multi-channel promotion
- [[User Generated Content]]: Customer content

### 5. Email Marketing
