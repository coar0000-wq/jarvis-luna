## What is Data Labeling?

### [[Data Labeling Foundation]]

**Core Definition**:
[[Data Labeling]] is the process of annotating raw data to make it understandable for machine learning algorithms.

#### [[Types of Data Being Labeled]]

**Image Labeling**:
- [[Object Detection]]: Identifying objects in images
- [[Image Classification]]: Categorizing images
- [[Semantic Segmentation]]: Marking specific areas
- [[Bounding Boxes]]: Drawing boundaries
- [[Facial Recognition]]: Identifying faces
- [[Scene Understanding]]: Describing environments
- [[Quality Assessment]]: Rating image quality
- [[Consistency Checking]]: Verifying accuracy

**Text Labeling**:
- [[Sentiment Analysis]]: Positive/negative/neutral
- [[Named Entity Recognition]]: Finding names, places
- [[Text Classification]]: Categorizing content
- [[Intent Identification]]: Understanding purpose
- [[Spam Detection]]: Identifying unwanted content
- [[Topic Tagging]]: Assigning categories
- [[Quality Rating]]: Evaluating text quality
- [[Relevance Scoring]]: Assessing usefulness

**Audio Labeling**:
- [[Speech Transcription]]: Converting speech to text
- [[Emotion Detection]]: Identifying emotional tone
- [[Accent Classification]]: Identifying speaker accent
- [[Noise Identification]]: Detecting background sounds
- [[Sound Event Tagging]]: Labeling specific sounds
- [[Language Identification]]: Detecting language
- [[Quality Assessment]]: Evaluating audio quality
- [[Speaker Identification]]: Identifying speakers

**Video Labeling**:
- [[Action Recognition]]: Identifying activities
- [[Scene Detection]]: Finding scene changes
- [[Object Tracking]]: Following objects through video
- [[Temporal Tagging]]: Marking time points
- [[Behavior Classification]]: Identifying behaviors
- [[Crowd Analysis]]: Analyzing groups
- [[Anomaly Detection]]: Finding unusual events
- [[Quality Control]]: Verifying accuracy
