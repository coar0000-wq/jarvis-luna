Claim Laundering: When Agent Decision Models Trust Records Over Reality
Agent decision models select actions in a single forward pass from inputs that combine environmental observations with the agent's own record of previous actions. When an action fails silently, these sources can conflict: the record reports success, but the environment shows that the step did not take effect. We ask which source determines the next decision.
