Measurement Validity and Representation Dynamics of Confidence Signals in Large Language Models
Whether a language model "knows what it knows" matters for trustworthy deployment and for deciding when it should abstain. Prior work usually measures this through one channel: verbalized confidence, a linear probe on hidden states, or agreement among sampled answers (semantic entropy), and often treats these as interchangeable. We compare all three in a pre-registered audit of five Qwen2.5 models
