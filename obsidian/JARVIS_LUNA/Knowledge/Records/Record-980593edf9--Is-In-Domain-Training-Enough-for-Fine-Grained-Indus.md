Is In-Domain Training Enough for Fine-Grained Industrial Anomaly Understanding?
A single multimodal large language model (MLLM) struggles to excel simultaneously at detection, localization, description, and reasoning in multimodal industrial anomaly understanding (MM-IAU). We show that in-domain training does not close this gap.
