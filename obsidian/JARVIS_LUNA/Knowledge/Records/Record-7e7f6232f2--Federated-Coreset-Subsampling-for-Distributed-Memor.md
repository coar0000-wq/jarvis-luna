Federated Coreset Subsampling for Distributed Memory Banks
In real-world scenarios, industrial processes can span multiple factories and goods are often produced at multiple sites, leading to a shared yet non-identical distribution of data across locations. To address this, we propose two federated approaches for i) combining existing coresets (Double Subsampling) and ii) creating new coresets (Federated Subsampling) in a distributed anomaly detection set
