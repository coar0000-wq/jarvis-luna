Knowledge Distillation of a Normalising Flow for Real-Time Anomaly Detection at LHC Level-1 Trigger
We present a new strategy for unsupervised anomaly detection at the hardware-based first stage of event processing (Level-1 trigger) of the Large Hadron Collider (LHC).
