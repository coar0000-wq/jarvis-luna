RCSD: Reinforcement and Continual Learning for Domain-Specific Speculative Decoding
Speculative decoding accelerates large language model inference by employing a compact draft model to propose candidate tokens that are verified in parallel by a target model. Conventional training relies on soft-label distillation, which forces the draft model to mimic the target's full output distribution, including high-entropy regions that degrade acceptance rates and increase early rejection
