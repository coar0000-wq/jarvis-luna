OLiVES: An Outdoor Low-Light Video Benchmark for Enhancement and Segmentation
Low-light video processing remains an underexplored problem in computer vision, primarily due to the scarcity of high-quality ground-truth data. Existing public datasets exhibit several limitations, including restricted motion diversity, limited scene coverage, weak degradations, reliance on auxiliary modalities such as event data, and misalignment between low-light inputs and ground truth.
