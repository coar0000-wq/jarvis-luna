When Less Compute Is More: Adaptive Early Exit Improves Pretrained Outlier Detection
Pretrained tabular foundation models process every dataset at a fixed depth, with inference costs growing with dataset size. To address this, we present the first study of depth-adaptive early-exit for pretrained outlier detection models.
