When to Stop Asking: Factor Coverage Bias and LLM Determinability Judgments with Underspecified Information in Medicine
Clinical decision-making often begins with underspecified information and requires not only gathering missing information but also knowing when to stop. This calls for judging determinability (whether the available information is sufficient for a decision) by considering whether missing information could change the conclusion, not simply the amount of available decision-relevant information.
