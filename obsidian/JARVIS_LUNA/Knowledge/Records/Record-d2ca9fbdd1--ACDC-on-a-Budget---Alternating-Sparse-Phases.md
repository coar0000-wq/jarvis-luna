AC/DC on a Budget - Alternating Sparse Phases
State-of-the-art neural network sparsification relies on alternating dense and sparse training phases. While computationally expensive, dense phases are critical for performance. Our empirical analysis reveals the mechanics behind this gain: upon reintroduction, previously masked parameters receive disproportionately large gradients that drive essential mask rewiring and parameter sign-flipping.
