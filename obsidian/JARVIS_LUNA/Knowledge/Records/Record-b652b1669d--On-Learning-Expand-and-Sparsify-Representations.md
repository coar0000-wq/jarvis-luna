On Learning Expand-and-Sparsify Representations
Expand-and-sparsify ($\texttt{EaS}$) representations lift an input to a much higher dimension through a linear map and keep only the $k$ largest coordinates. Existing theory builds that map at random and needs the expanded dimension to grow exponentially in the input dimension, which puts the construction out of practical reach. We ask what is gained by learning the map by gradient descent instead
