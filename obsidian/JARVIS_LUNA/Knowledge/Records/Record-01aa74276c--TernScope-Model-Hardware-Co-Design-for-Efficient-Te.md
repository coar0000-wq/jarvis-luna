TernScope: Model-Hardware Co-Design for Efficient Ternary Endoscopic Segmentation
Edge medical segmentation must reconcile small model size, high accuracy, and fast inference. TernScope addresses this tradeoff through model--hardware co-design, learning a compact segmentation network whose low-bit operators also simplify hardware execution. Split-channel large-kernel blocks preserve spatial context with limited computation, while branch fusion removes redundant inference paths.
