Spatially Gated Demographic Steering for Debiasing Multi-Person Text-to-Image Generation
Text-to-Image models reproduce societal stereotypes, and existing mitigation applies one demographic correction to the whole scene. In multi-person scene generation this creates a trade-off: global steering improves aggregate balance only by homogenizing the people within an image.
