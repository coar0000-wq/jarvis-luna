Executing Causal Structure Learning with Linear-Attention Transformers
Transformers can execute algorithms on data given in their input. We ask whether they can do the same for causal discovery. We study a standard continuous method that repeatedly updates a candidate causal graph while enforcing acyclicity. We explicitly construct a fixed-weight transformer whose forward pass exactly reproduces one update of this method, so repeated blocks reproduce its optimization
