Two Global Crops Suffice: Locating Semantic Emergence in DINO-Style Self-Supervised Learning
Self-supervised vision transformers trained with DINO-style objectives exhibit striking emergent semantic representation quality across visual tasks, yet the mechanisms underlying this behavior remain unclear.
