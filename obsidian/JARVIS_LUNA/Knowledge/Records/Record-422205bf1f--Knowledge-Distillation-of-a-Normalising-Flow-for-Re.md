Knowledge Distillation of a Normalising Flow for Real-Time Anomaly Detection at the CMS Level-1 Trigger
We present a new strategy for unsupervised anomaly detection at the hardware-based first stage of LHC event processing (Level-1 Trigger). A normalising flow trained exclusively on Standard Model (SM) events provides a teacher anomaly score based on its exact negative log-likelihood, which is distilled into a compact neural network suitable for FPGA deployment. The teacher reaches state-of-the-art
