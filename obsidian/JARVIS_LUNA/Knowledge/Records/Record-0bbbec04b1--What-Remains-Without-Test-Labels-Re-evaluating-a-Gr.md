What Remains Without Test Labels? Re-evaluating a Graph-Level Anomaly Detection Benchmark
Graph-level anomaly detectors are trained only on normal graphs, yet benchmarks may use test labels for model selection. We examine whether UB-GOLD results reused by later papers support label-free detection.
