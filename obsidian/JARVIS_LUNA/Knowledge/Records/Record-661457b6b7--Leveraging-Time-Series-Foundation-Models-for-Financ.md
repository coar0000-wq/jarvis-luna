Leveraging Time-Series Foundation Models for Financial Crime Detection
Financial crime detection requires identifying illicit activity within large transaction networks, where suspicious behaviour is rare and unfolds over time. Existing graph-based methods capture relationships among accounts but typically underuse the temporal behaviour encoded in account transaction histories. We introduce Temporal-Context Edge Enrichment (TCEE), a framework that addresses this gap
