SlackSwap: Bandwidth-Aware Activation Swapping and Recomputation for Memory-Efficient DNN Training
Swapping saved activations to host memory once enabled GPUs to train models that exceeded their device-memory capacity when compute throughput and host-link bandwidth were roughly balanced. For example, on a 2015 Titan X, PCIe bandwidth was sufficient to offload all VGG-16 activations during the forward pass. This balance no longer holds.
