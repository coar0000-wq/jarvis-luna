FIRE3D: Feed-forward Interactive 3D Scene Reconstruction Within A Minute
We present FIRE3D, a unified framework that takes a single RGB image or casual RGB video and transforms it into simulation-ready 3D scene assets for games and interactive applications in under a minute. At the core of FIRE3D is a feed-forward, end-to-end network that predicts a compositional scene representation from posed RGB-D observations estimated from the RGB capture, including the 6-DoF pose
