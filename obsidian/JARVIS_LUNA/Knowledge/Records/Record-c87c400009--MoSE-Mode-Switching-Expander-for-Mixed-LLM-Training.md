MoSE: Mode-Switching Expander for Mixed LLM Training and Inference
AI clusters increasingly run large language model (LLM) inference and training on the same fabric. Prefill-decode (P-D) disaggregation creates key-value (KV) cache transfers between prefill and decode groups, whereas training collectives and all-to-all traffic benefit from near-uniform global connectivity. A static sparse topology can therefore be poorly matched to one of the two traffic patterns.
