Spherical Fixed-Point Reasoners: Adaptive Loop Halting on the Hypersphere
Looped (weight-tied) transformers enable adaptive computation by allocating more iterations to harder inputs. Fixed-Point Reasoners (FPRM) achieve this with a damped fixed-point solver, but operate in an unbounded Euclidean residual space with coupled rescaling and relative-norm halting. We introduce Spherical Fixed-Point Reasoners (SFPRM), which perform the same computation on the unit hyperspher
