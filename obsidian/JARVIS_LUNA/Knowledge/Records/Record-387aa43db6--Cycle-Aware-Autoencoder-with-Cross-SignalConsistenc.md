Cycle-Aware Autoencoder with Cross-SignalConsistency for Railway Door Anomaly Detection
Passenger access doors are safety-critical subsystems in railway vehicles, yet detecting abnormal door behavior in real operation is challenging because faults are rare, diverse, and often unlabeled. This paper addresses railway door condition monitoring as a cycle-level unsupervised anomaly detection problem, where each complete opening-dwell-closing cycle is treated as a single monitoring unit.
