Virtual Head Attention
The design of attention mechanisms in long-context large language models faces an ``impossible triangle'' among KV cache size, training and prefill FLOPs, and model quality. GQA achieves strong quality but incurs high KV cache overhead; alternative approaches such as MLA and MFA can compress the cache, but MLA suffers from quality loss while MFA substantially increases training and prefill compute
