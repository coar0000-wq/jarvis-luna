Fair Regression under Approximate Demographic Parity
Exact demographic parity requires the same prediction distribution across sensitive groups. In regression, this can be too restrictive and reduce predictive accuracy. We instead bound every pairwise Wasserstein distance between group-specific prediction distributions by a chosen tolerance, allowing the groups to differ while keeping fairness tunable.
